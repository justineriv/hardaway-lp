What Non-Surgical Procedures Involve

Non-surgical cosmetic procedures alter your appearance without major surgery. They include chemical peels, radio-frequency skin tightening, laser treatment and various fillers, along with injectable neurotoxin and similar treatments. With today’s advances, more women are choosing them to get the natural look they have always wanted.

What to expect

Your Treatment Options, Explained

  1. 01

    Fraxel and laser hair removal

    Think of your skin as a photograph in need of restoration. Just as a damaged painting is restored one small area at a time, Fraxel Laser Treatment improves your appearance by affecting only a fraction of your skin at a time, with thousands of tiny microscopic laser spots.

    Laser hair removal uses the latest technology to reduce unwanted hair over a course of treatments. It is a long-term reduction rather than one-and-done removal, and how many sessions you need depends on your hair and skin — Dr. Hardaway sets that out at your consultation.

  2. 02

    Spider veins and dermal fillers

    Spider veins can be treated with sclerotherapy, injecting the vein with a solution that causes it to disappear over three to six weeks, or with lasers that use light to close the vein. Dr. Hardaway will evaluate your veins and decide which treatment is better for you. Both cause minimal discomfort and downtime.

    As we age the face develops wrinkles through loss of skin elasticity, and the skin begins to lose volume. Dermal fillers are injections that plump the face to correct that — increasing lip size, replenishing volume in the cheeks, and filling fine lines, smile lines and the nasolabial folds.
