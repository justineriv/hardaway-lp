At a glance

What a Nose Job Involves

Rhinoplasty reshapes and resizes the nose to create a more balanced facial appearance, and it can correct functional problems at the same time. Whether you want to refine your profile, fix breathing difficulties, or both, Dr. Hardaway builds a surgical plan around your own goals.

Open or closed technique

Open rhinoplasty gives greater visibility through a small incision along the columella. Closed rhinoplasty places incisions inside the nostrils, leaving no visible scars.

Appearance and function

The same operation can adjust size, shape and proportion while correcting a deviated septum or other breathing issues.

Lasting structural change

Rhinoplasty reshapes the underlying structure rather than masking it, so the change does not wear off the way a temporary treatment does. A face still changes with age, and Dr. Hardaway sets out what to expect at your consultation.

What to expect

Your Rhinoplasty, Step by Step

  1. 01

    The surgical plan

    At your consultation Dr. Hardaway discusses your goals, assesses your needs and develops a tailored plan. Rhinoplasty is performed under general or local anesthesia, using precise incisions to access and reshape the nasal structure.

  2. 02

    Open or closed

    She offers both techniques: open rhinoplasty, with a small incision along the columella for greater visibility, and closed rhinoplasty, with incisions inside the nostrils that leave no visible scars.

  3. 03

    Recovery and results

    Most patients return to work within one to two weeks after surgery, though some swelling and bruising may last for several months. Final results become visible within a year. The structural change itself is lasting rather than temporary, though a face continues to change with age.
